What I Study

I'm interested in understanding how galaxies form and evolve throughout the history of the universe. I combine photometric and spectroscopic observations with stellar population synthesis (SPS) modeling to uncover the star formation histories of galaxies. I am extremley interested in galaxies in the early universe (z > 5) and how their star formation is highly stochastic.

Surveys

POPPIES

I lead the image data reduction and photometric catalog construction for POPPIES, a JWST pure-parallel NIRCam wide-field slitless spectroscopy (WFSS) survey. A paper describing the survey catalogs is in preparation.

Using POPPIES, I am also investigating why star formation in the early universe (\(z \sim 7\text{--}12\)) appears so stochastic. By combining photometric and spectroscopic observations with stellar population synthesis (SPS) modeling, I aim to constrain the star formation histories of these early galaxies. More to come on this work! ;)

PASSAGE (Coming Soon!)

Coming soon! I'll be joining IPAC for the visiting graduate student research fellowship in 2027 to work with PASSAGE, a pure parallel JWST NIRISS WFSS survey. More to come as for this work as it develops!

SQuIGGLE

As an undergraduate researcher at the University of Pittsburgh, I studied gas-rich neighboring galaxies around post-starburst galaxies (PSBs) in the SQuIGGLE survey. Using ALMA CO(2–1) observations, I identified gas-rich companions and combined photometry from the HSC and DECaLS surveys with Bagpipes and Prospector to model their star formation histories.

I also compared these results with the UniverseMachine to investigate how commonly post-starburst galaxies have nearby companions. Check out this work in the Publications tab!

Other Research Experience

STEPUP

The Study of Transiting Extra-Solar Planets at the University of Pittsburgh (STEPUP) is a research group that uses the 24-inch and 16-inch Keeler telescopes at Allegheny Observatory to observe known and candidate exoplanets for the TESS and Exoplanet Watch projects. As group leader, I taught undergraduate students about time-domain astronomy, data reduction, and interpreting observational results. I also participated in public outreach at Allegheny Observatory.
